Perchseal is an evening lockup book for backyard keepers who lose track of headcount after free-ranging. Each night you mark every banded bird home, photograph an empty perch if someone is missing, and seal when the perch matches the birds you still keep. It is a personal census, not a farm ledger of mash or eggs.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows a dark system launch screen with no words, then a brief empty wait. On a physical device with no flock yet, four onboarding pages follow. 1 of 4: Lock up the flock. Each evening the perch should match the birds you still keep. Next. 2 of 4: Scan the band. A known wing band marks that bird home. One manual mark is allowed each week. Next. 3 of 4: Photograph the gap. If someone is missing, photograph the empty perch before you seal. Next. 4 of 4: Seal the night. Seal when every bird is counted, or the empty perch has a photo. Continue. Skip on any page, or Continue on the last page, finishes onboarding and opens Tonight.
